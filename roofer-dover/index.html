A homeowner's hiring checklist

Use these points to get ready for a conversation with a Roofer Dover residents may contact:

Questions people ask about Roofer Dover

When should I call a roofer about my home?

Call when you notice a roof concern, water entering the home, or a change you can't assess safely. Describe what you've observed rather than trying to inspect the roof yourself.

What should I ask before hiring a roofer?

Ask what the recommended work addresses, what it includes, and how it fits your needs and budget. If an answer is unclear, ask for a simpler explanation before making a decision.

Can I prepare photos for a roofing discussion?

Yes, photos can help explain what you've seen if you take them from a safe location. Don't climb onto the roof or use an unsafe position to get a closer image.
