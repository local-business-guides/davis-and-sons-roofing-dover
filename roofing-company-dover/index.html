What to ask and prepare

You don’t need to arrive with a technical diagnosis. A few notes can make the discussion easier and help keep the focus on your property.

  • Write down the areas of the roof or home that concern you.
  • Note when you first noticed a change or problem, if you know.
  • Gather questions about the work being considered and its scope.
  • Decide what budget considerations you want to discuss.
  • Be ready to explain what outcome you’re hoping for.

Ask what the proposed work is intended to address and whether there are decisions you should make before moving ahead. If you have photos or other information that help explain what you noticed, mention them. Don’t climb onto the roof to collect details. If you’re unsure whether a concern needs professional attention, it’s sensible to call and describe it.
