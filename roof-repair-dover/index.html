What to have ready, and what to avoid

A few simple details can help you explain the situation without putting yourself at risk. Before calling, consider gathering:

  • The part of the roof or home where you noticed the concern.
  • The date or approximate time you first observed it.
  • Notes about any changes you’ve seen since then.
  • Questions about the repair scope and the intended result.
  • Any budget considerations you want to raise.

Avoid climbing onto the roof to inspect it or attempting a fix if you’re not equipped to do that safely. A quick patch or an assumed solution may not address the issue you’re seeing. If you’re uncertain about the condition of the roof or what to do next, contact a roofing professional and describe the concern from a safe place.
