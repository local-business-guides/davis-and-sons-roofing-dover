Questions and preparation before hiring

A little preparation can make the conversation more useful. Write down what you’ve noticed, where you’ve seen it, and whether it has happened more than once. If you have notes or paperwork from earlier roofing work, keep them nearby. You can then ask focused questions such as:

  • What should be assessed before recommending work?
  • What does the proposed scope include?
  • Are there options that account for my budget?
  • What should I expect before the work is scheduled?
  • Who should I contact if I have another question?

Be cautious about agreeing to a scope you don’t understand. Don’t assume that a quick answer replaces an assessment, and don’t leave important questions until after you’ve made a decision. If you see active leaking or damage that appears to be worsening, contact a roofing professional to discuss what to do next.
